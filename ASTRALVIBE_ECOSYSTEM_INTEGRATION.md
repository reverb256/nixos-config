# AstralVibe Ecosystem Integration with Reverb-OS

## 🌌 **Overview**

The AstralVibe ecosystem consists of interconnected systems that form the foundation of Reverb-OS, each playing a specialized role in the "Apple-Easy + Bitcoin-Powered" infrastructure platform:

- **AstralVibe.ca**: The philosophical and consciousness-driven platform foundation
